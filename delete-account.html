Ridegain — Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: August 10, 2026 · App: Ridegain (com.ridegain.app) · Developer: Ridegain

This page explains how to delete your Ridegain account and the data associated with it. See also our Privacy Policy.

Deleting your account from within the App

  1. Open the Ridegain app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open the Account section (you must be signed in).
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately. Your Strava authorization is revoked at the same time, and the training data held on that device is erased as well.

Requesting deletion by email

If you can no longer access the App, email mitkcycling@gmail.com from the address associated with the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Account deletion request". We will process the request and confirm by reply, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Account identifier (Google sub, our user ID)Deleted immediately
Device information (device ID, platform, push token, last access time)Deleted immediately
Strava connection (athlete ID, encrypted access and refresh tokens, scopes)Deleted immediately, and the authorization is revoked on Strava's side
Settings and metrics backup (FTP and body-weight history, custom workouts, notifications, unit and language preferences)Deleted immediately
Rides awaiting upload, including raw waveformDeleted immediately
Incoming Strava eventsDeleted immediately
Training data on the device you used to delete the account (ride history and raw waveforms)Erased immediately, and you are signed out

No backup copies are retained after deletion. We keep no data beyond the above on our servers.

What is not deleted

  • Data on your other devices. Deleting your account erases the training data on the device you performed the deletion from, but any other device that still has the App installed keeps its local copy. Use the App's data-deletion feature or uninstall the App on those devices as well.
  • Activities already uploaded to Strava. These belong to your Strava account. Delete them yourself on Strava.

Using the App without an account

Signing in is optional. Without an account, Ridegain records and stores your rides entirely on your device, and nothing is sent to our servers.
