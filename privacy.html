Ridegain Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Ridegain ("the App") is an indoor cycling training app provided by its developer, Ridegain ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Overview

Your training records are stored on your device. In addition, only if you choose to sign in (with Apple or Google), a server we operate (Cloudflare Workers + D1) holds the minimum data needed for syncing across your devices and for the Strava integration.

We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number. Sign-in gives us only the subject identifier issued by Apple or Google; no information that directly identifies you reaches us.

The App displays no advertising and uses no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs whatsoever.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Location (GPS) — we do not request location data from Strava either
  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID)
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking

5. Permissions

  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): To discover and connect to sensors such as heart-rate monitors, power meters, and smart trainers, and to obtain exercise data.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To deliver sync results and service announcements. We may also send silent notifications, which are not shown on screen, to detect that the App has been removed from a device (see section 7).

6. Strava Integration (Optional)

Only if you choose to enable Strava integration:

  • Upload: Indoor rides recorded in the App are uploaded to your Strava account.
  • Import: We read your Strava cycling activities and their time, power, cadence, and heart-rate streams, and use them for the analytics shown in the App (TSS, power curve, CTL/ATL/TSB, and so on). We do not request location (GPS) data.
  • How it works: The tokens obtained through OAuth are stored encrypted on our server and never reach your device. New activities arrive via Strava webhook notifications; we do not poll the API.
  • Data obtained from Strava is used only for the analytics inside the App and is never shared with third parties. Data on Strava itself is handled under Strava's Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect Strava at any time within the App. Disconnecting deletes the tokens we hold and also revokes the authorization on Strava's side.

7. Deleting Your Data

  • Disconnecting Strava within the App deletes the stored tokens and revokes the authorization on Strava.
  • Deleting your account within the App erases everything listed in section 3 and revokes the Strava authorization.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on that device. In addition, once we detect that the App has been removed from all of your devices, we automatically revoke the Strava authorization.
  • To delete activities already uploaded to Strava, please do so yourself on Strava.

8. Processors and Where Data Lives

We use the following services, only for the purposes described in this policy. None of them are permitted to sell your data or use it for advertising.

  • Cloudflare (Workers / D1) — server processing and data storage
  • Apple / Google (sign-in) — authentication; we receive only an identifier
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) / Apple Push Notification service (iOS) — push notification delivery

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age set by your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Regional Laws

  • EU / UK (GDPR): What we store is limited to section 3, and all of it is necessary to provide the features of the App (performance of a contract). You can erase it yourself at any time by deleting your account in the App.
  • California, USA (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell your personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy as needed. If we make significant changes, we will announce them on this page.
