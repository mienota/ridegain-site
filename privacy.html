Ridegain Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Ridegain ("the App") is an indoor cycling training app provided by its developer, Ridegain ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Overview

If you do not sign in, the App stores your data only on your device.

If you choose to sign in to an account, we store some of your data on servers we operate (Cloudflare Workers / D1) in order to provide Strava integration and synchronization across your devices. Section 3 lists exactly what is stored.

However, in order to deliver announcements from us (maintenance, incidents, and new features), the App makes an anonymous request to our servers regardless of whether you are signed in. That request sends only your display language and the app version; it contains nothing that identifies you (no account identifier, device identifier, or session). We keep no record of who received an announcement.

The App displays no advertising and uses no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs whatsoever.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Location (GPS) — as explained in Section 5, location permissions are declared solely to allow Bluetooth scanning; we never obtain, record, or transmit your location
  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID)
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking

5. Permissions

  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_ADMIN): To discover and connect to sensors such as heart-rate monitors, power meters, and smart trainers, and to obtain exercise data.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Declared only because older versions of Android require this permission in order to scan for Bluetooth devices. The App never obtains, stores, or transmits your location.
  • Storage (READ / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): To let you export and import backup files.
  • Internet: For Strava integration and synchronization.

6. Strava Integration (Optional)

Only if you choose to enable Strava integration:

  • Your workout records (activities) in the App are uploaded to your Strava account.
  • Your activities on Strava are imported into the App.
  • The tokens obtained through authorization (OAuth) are stored encrypted on our servers (Section 3). This is necessary so that we can receive updates from Strava while the App is not open.
  • Data sent to Strava is handled under Strava's Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect Strava at any time within the App. Disconnecting deletes the tokens from our servers and revokes the authorization on Strava's side.

7. Push Tokens and Uninstall Detection

When you sign in, your device's push token (on Android, Firebase Cloud Messaging) is registered with our servers.

We use it primarily to detect that you have uninstalled the App. When an uninstall is detected, we deactivate that device's registration and automatically revoke the Strava authorization, so that no abandoned token is left behind without you having to do anything.

9. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your data at any time. See Account Deletion for step-by-step instructions.

  • Deleting your account (from within the App's settings) erases all of your data on our servers — account identifier, device information, Strava connection, backup, rides awaiting upload, and incoming events — and revokes the Strava authorization. It also erases the training data held on the device you performed the deletion from and signs you out.
  • Disconnecting Strava deletes the stored tokens and revokes the authorization on Strava's side.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on your device. Server-side data is deactivated through the detection described in Section 7, but to erase it with certainty, delete your account before uninstalling.
  • To delete activities already uploaded to Strava, please do so yourself on Strava.
  • You may also request deletion by email (see Section 12).

10. Regional Laws

  • EU / UK (GDPR): Our legal bases for processing the data in Section 3 are performance of a contract (providing the App's functionality) and your consent (Strava integration). You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, and portability. The App's backup export feature lets you obtain your data in a machine-readable format.
  • California, USA (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell or share (for cross-context behavioral advertising) your personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age set by your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy as needed. If we make significant changes, we will announce them on this page.
